01 Who we are

We believe young people should not only use technology — they should build with it.

Youth in Technology and Engineering (YITEISM) is a youth-led non-governmental organization working to expand access to practical STEM education, innovation and meaningful opportunities for young people.

We create spaces where youth can learn by doing, turn ideas into solutions, develop leadership skills and participate in shaping the future of their communities.
